## Question

What labeling manual makes company target-condition polarity, macro-indicator direction, aspect applicability, evidence sufficiency, mixed signals, negation, temporality, reported speech, sarcasm, and cross-target spillovers consistent enough for one annotator? Draft a rough manual, apply it to difficult examples spanning every source and both target families, and use the human's reactions to revise ontology version 1 and the rules before they are frozen.

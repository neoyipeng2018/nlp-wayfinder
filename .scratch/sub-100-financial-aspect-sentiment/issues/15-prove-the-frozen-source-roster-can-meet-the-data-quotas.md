# Prove the frozen source roster can meet the data quotas

GitHub: https://github.com/neoyipeng2018/nlp-wayfinder/issues/16

Type: task
Status: open
Blocked by: 09

## Question

Run a bounded feasibility audit, not full dataset collection. For each frozen source, inspect at least 20 candidate passages, including at least five macro-indicator candidates. Record the source owner, access path, terms snapshot, rights lane, target family, likely aspect, likely class, event group, duplicate status, and acceptance or rejection reason. Test issuer-hosted earnings-call transcripts and audio as separate acquisition cases. Estimate the accepted yield and the work needed to meet the frozen training, development, and blind quotas. Can the source roster meet every quota under the access, time, event, entity, and contamination rules with no unapproved external spend? Treat a clear term that prohibits collection or model evaluation as a build gate. Treat other unresolved rights as a lane or release limit, not as a stop rule for the research experiment. Report each failed source or quota as a feasibility gate.
